Privacy Policy – YouTube Reports

YouTube Reports is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: read-only analytics and video information of the owner's own YouTube channel, using the scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly.
  • What it does with it: creates a daily summary report that is stored in the owner's private repository.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Changes: the tool cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
